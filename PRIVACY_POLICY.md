# Privacy Policy for GramSeva : Kisan Sathi

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

**ChittorTech** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **GramSeva : Kisan Sathi** mobile application (the "App"). This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone who uses our App.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**GramSeva : Kisan Sathi** is built to serve farmers with daily mandi bhav (market prices), local weather forecasts, and government agricultural scheme information.

- **No Personal Account Required:** We do not require users to register an account, log in, or provide personal details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **Location Data:** The App may optionally request device location permission strictly to determine your local tehsil/district for localized weather forecasts and relevant mandi market rates. This location data is processed locally on your device and is **never** collected, stored on our servers, or shared with third parties.
- **No Financial Information:** We do not collect bank details, credit card numbers, or any financial transaction data.
- **Device Identifiers & Analytics:** We do not use third-party tracking SDKs or cross-app advertising identifiers.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Data Sources

Our app fetches publicly available agricultural data to assist farmers:
- **Mandi Bhav / Market Rates:** Sourced from public agricultural data portals (Government of India / Agmarknet open data).
- **Weather Information:** Fetched via standard public meteorological APIs (e.g., Open-Meteo).

These services do not receive any personally identifiable information from your usage of the App.

---

### 3. Advertising

The current version of the App does not display advertisements and does not integrate any third-party ad networks.

---

### 4. Data Security

We value your trust. Because the App operates without collecting personal user information, your private data is never at risk of unauthorized access or disclosure from our servers.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for farmers and adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting.
